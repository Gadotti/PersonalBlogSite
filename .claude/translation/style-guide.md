# EN (American) style guide — Eduardo Gadotti's blog

Owner decisions: preserve the PT tone of each post; keep Brazil-specific references and gloss them on first mention; industry-standard English terms for tech/security; tags keep the PT names.

## Voice
- First person as in the original ("I", "we"). Match the PT register: conversational stays conversational, analytical stays analytical.
- Contractions are fine when the PT tone is informal; avoid them when it is formal.
- Prefer active voice and concrete verbs. Short and medium sentences; split long Portuguese periods.
- Keep the author's metaphors when they work in English; otherwise use an equivalent image of the same force and say so in the report.

## Localization
- American spelling and vocabulary: color, organization, analyze, license (noun and verb), program.
- Dates: "September 5, 2026". Numbers: 1,000.50 (not 1.000,50). Currency: keep "R$"; never invent exchange rates.
- Brazilian institutions/laws: keep the name, add an English gloss on first use.
- Idioms: translate the meaning, never the words. If an idiom has no equivalent, use a plain descriptive rendering.
- Avoid filler connectives that are Portuguese habits ("In other words", "Therefore", "Thus", "Moreover" opening many sentences). Vary or drop them.
- Gender-neutral wording when referring to people generically.

## Headings and titles
- Follow the case used by existing EN posts (sentence case after a colon, as in "Efficient security: Investing smartly, spending less").
- Titles should be natural, searchable English, not a mirror of the PT structure.

## Formatting
- Keep Markdown structure identical to the PT source.
- Don't translate code, commands, identifiers, URLs, paths.

## False friends (BR-PT → EN) quick list
atualmente = currently (not "actually") · eventualmente = occasionally/possibly (not "eventually") · pretender = intend (not "pretend") · assistir = watch/attend · realizar = carry out/accomplish (not "realize") · aplicação = application, or investment by context · evidência = evidence (mass noun) · resumir = summarize · suportar = support/handle (not "suffer") · domínio = domain or mastery by context.
